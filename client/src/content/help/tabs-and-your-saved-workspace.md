Each Workspace tab holds a search and its pinned items. A tab takes its name from its first
search unless you’ve already renamed it.

The app saves your workspace in this browser on this device. Searches still contact the server.
Clearing this site’s browser data deletes the saved workspace.

- Closing a tab deletes its pinned items.
- **Clear workspace** deletes all tabs and starts with a blank one after you confirm.
- If you open the app in a second window and change something there, the first window stops
  saving and asks you to reload, so the two don't overwrite each other.

The FDA and NADAC pages under **Search** also save their criteria and shared pins in this browser.

After a search loads, choose **Add search to workspace** in the search panel beside the filter
controls. The new tab copies the applied search terms, dates, filters, FDA **Show** setting and
all shared pins. The copied dates become a fixed period you can change in the tab. Edits in
either place stay in that copy, and the tab may load results again from the server.
