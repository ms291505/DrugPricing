Each Workspace tab holds a search and its pinned items. A tab takes its name from its search,
and **Clear search** gives it back its default name. Once you rename a tab, it keeps your name.

The app saves your workspace in this browser on this device: each tab’s search terms, dates,
and filters, and your pinned charts and their prices. Anyone who uses this browser profile can
see them, because the app reopens your tabs when you come back. To delete them, close a tab, use
**Clear workspace** in the Workspace menu, or clear this site’s data in your browser. If you use
a private window, your browser clears your saved workspace when you close all private windows.

- Closing a tab deletes its pinned items.
- **Clear workspace** deletes all tabs and starts with a blank one after you confirm.
- If you open the app in a second window and change a search, tab, or pin there, the first
  window stops saving and asks you to reload, so the two don't overwrite each other. Settings
  changes apply in every window without a reload.

Each search sends its terms and dates to the server, which uses them to find prices and doesn’t
keep them.

The FDA and NADAC pages under **Search** save their search and shared pins for this browser tab
only. To keep a search longer, add it to the workspace: after a search loads, choose
**Add search to workspace** in the search panel beside the filter controls. The new tab copies
the applied search terms, dates, filters, FDA **Show** setting, and all shared pins. The copied
dates become a fixed period you can change in the tab. Edits in either place stay in that copy,
and the tab may load results again from the server.
