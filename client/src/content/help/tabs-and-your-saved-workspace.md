Each workspace tab holds a search and its pinned items. The tab takes its name from your search
unless you rename it. If you haven’t renamed it, **Clear search** gives it back its default name.

## What’s saved and who can see it

You don’t need an account, and the app doesn’t ask for any personal information. It saves your
workspace in this browser on this device: each workspace tab’s search terms, dates, and filters,
and your pinned charts and their prices. Anyone who uses this browser profile can see them,
because the app reopens your workspace tabs when you come back. If you use a private window,
your browser clears your saved workspace when you close all private windows.

Each search sends its terms and dates to the server, which uses them to find prices and doesn’t
keep them.

## Closing tabs and clearing the workspace

Closing a workspace tab deletes its search and pins. If you close the browser tab, your workspace
will still be here when you return in the same browser profile, unless you used a private window
or cleared this site’s data in your browser.

- **Clear workspace** in the Workspace menu deletes all workspace tabs and starts with a blank
  one after you confirm. Clearing this site’s data in your browser also deletes them.
- If you open the app in a second browser tab or window and change a search, workspace tab, or
  pin there, the first one stops saving and asks you to reload, so the two don’t overwrite each
  other. Settings changes apply in every browser tab and window without a reload.

## Keeping a search from the Search pages

Under **Search**, your searches and shared pins stay in this browser tab. To keep a copy between
visits, choose **Add search to workspace** in the search panel beside the filter controls after
a search loads. The new workspace tab has its own copy of the search and pins: the applied search
terms, dates, filters, FDA **Show** setting, and all shared pins. The copied dates become a fixed
period you can change in the tab. Edits in either place stay in that copy, and the workspace tab
may load results again from the server.
