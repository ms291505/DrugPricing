The Workspace holds tabs, each with its own search and pinned charts. Tabs are named after
their first search, and you can rename them.

Your workspace is saved in this browser, on this device. Saved workspace state is kept locally; searches still contact the server. There is
no account, and it won't follow you to another browser or phone. Clearing your browser's site
data deletes it.

- Closing a tab deletes its pinned charts.
- **Clear workspace** deletes every tab and starts over. It asks twice.
- If you open the app in a second window and change something there, the first window stops
  saving and asks you to reload, so the two don't overwrite each other.

The standalone search pages also save their criteria and pinned charts in this browser.

**Add search to workspace**, at the bottom of a standalone FDA or NADAC search page, opens an independent tab
with the applied search, dates, filters, FDA Products/Packages choice and all pinned charts
from the Search area. Load a search successfully first. Text you haven't submitted, selected
rows and scroll position stay on the original page. The copy keeps the current dates as a
fixed period; you can change them in its search panel. Later edits to either search or its
pins don't affect the other. Results may load again from the server.
