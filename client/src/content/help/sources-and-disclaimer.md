The app uses two public data sources.

- [National Average Drug Acquisition Cost (NADAC) — CMS](https://www.medicaid.gov/medicaid/prescription-drugs/pharmacy-pricing)
- [National Drug Code Directory — FDA](https://www.fda.gov/drugs/drug-approvals-and-databases/national-drug-code-directory)

CMS’s pharmacy pricing page links to survey information and data field definitions.

DrugPricing is an independent learning project. It isn’t affiliated with or endorsed by CMS or the FDA. NADAC estimates what retail community pharmacies pay to acquire drugs. It doesn’t show what patients pay at the pharmacy, and it isn’t a retail price or a reimbursement rate. The data may be incomplete or out of date. DrugPricing does not provide medical, financial, or pricing advice.

Retail community pharmacies submit invoices voluntarily, so the estimates reflect the invoices
submitted rather than every pharmacy’s costs. CMS excludes discounts and rebates that don’t
appear at the drug line-item level on an invoice. See the
[CMS NADAC methodology](https://www.medicaid.gov/medicaid-chip-program-information/by-topics/prescription-drugs/ful-nadac-downloads/nadacmethodology.pdf).
