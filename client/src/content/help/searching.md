- Enter at least five characters in a name or description field, or at least five digits
  in **NDC**. Choose **Search** or press Enter.
- A search matches anywhere in the text, regardless of capitalization. For example,
  `loratadine` also matches `desloratadine`.
- If a search returns no matches, check the spelling or try a shorter term of at least five
  characters.
- For a brand and its unbranded version in NADAC, search each name separately. For example,
  search `humira` and `adalimumab`.
- **Search** becomes available when you edit the terms or can retry after an error. Dates and
  filters apply automatically.
- **Clear search** empties the search fields and puts the dates and filters back to their
  defaults, so you can start over. Pinned items stay.
