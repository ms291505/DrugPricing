Look up a drug’s National Average Drug Acquisition Cost (NADAC), compare products and packages, and see when the benchmark changed and by how much.

For example, if your plan’s PBM report shows different ingredient costs for two drugs, chart both over the same dates to see whether NADAC shows a similar gap, and whether it was there a year ago. [Things to try](/help/things-to-try) walks through it.

- **FDA search** finds products and packages by brand or generic name and shows matching NADAC prices.
- **NADAC search** finds package price histories by description or NDC, including packages absent from the FDA directory.

NADAC estimates what retail community pharmacies pay to buy drugs. It doesn’t show what patients pay at the pharmacy. See [What the prices mean](/help/what-the-prices-mean).
