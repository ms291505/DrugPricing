**Price Activity** describes each package over the search's dates. Stable means its lowest and
highest price were less than 5% apart; Changed means a range of at least 5%. The comparison is
rounded to a tenth of a percent at the cutoff. A package with just one observation has
insufficient history. Product activity comes from its packages, never from their average.

A significant change is a move of at least 10% between consecutive package observations. Percents
are compared to a tenth of a percent, so a drop from $1.00 to $0.90 counts as 10%. A change from a
price of $0 has no percent: it counts as a rise at any percent and is shown as "from $0".

The **Notable price changes** card groups these events by date, pricing unit and direction. It
sits with live comparison and event charts in the responsive card grid below the results table.
The **Chart latest prices** and **Chart price histories** controls are directly below the table
and appear before the cards. **Package details** lists each package's price before and after, and
its change in percent and dollars per unit; it starts open on wide screens. When observations are
more than a week apart, the group names both dates; the change may have happened anywhere in that
span.

To look for smaller or larger changes, enter a percent beside **Scan price changes** and press it
(or Enter). Each scan adds its own card, such as "Price changes of 25% or more", covering the
search's dates and filters. The percent must be at least 5, with at most one decimal; lower
percents would list most of a result. Above 100% only rises can match. A scan that finds nothing
adds no card and says so beside the button. Scan cards last until you close them or the prices
change: a new search, or new dates or filters, removes them.

Every price change card has **Pin** and **Close**. Closing Notable price changes hides it until the
prices change. **Pin** keeps a copy of the list in the Pinned section (see Pinning and comparing).

Each package row has a **Chart this change** line-chart button. It opens that package's own
unpinned chart card after the price change cards, over the search's dates. A solid line marks the
change, and the readout rests on its date until you point at another. Several packages can have
charts open at once, and a change listed by two cards has one chart, which both buttons show as
open. Collapsing or closing a card leaves its charts open. **Pin** keeps a copy, and **Close** or
the same button again closes the chart. Close returns focus to the package button, or to a price
change card's heading when that button is hidden. New prices close all event charts. To see every
result together, use **Chart price histories**.

Line charts mark significant changes in the dates they show. Several series changing on the
same date share one label; their per-package changes are in the search's Notable price changes card or at Package level, and a
package chart's **View data** has its exact prices.
A product marker means one or more packages changed, not that its average made that change.
Older product pins without package identities cannot show those package markers. A pinned
line chart's menu can turn **Show price changes** off or on.

The activity label names the largest significant step by dollar amount, with the earlier step
winning a tie. Otherwise it describes the net movement, or a dip or spike that returned near
the starting price. The small package sparkline is a cue; each row has its own bounded scale.
Product rows have no sparkline because one package would not represent the others. Sort Price
Activity to put significant changes first, then other changed, stable and insufficient history.

A flag is arithmetic, not news. See "Five ways a price can mislead" before reading much into
one, especially for generics in 2024.
