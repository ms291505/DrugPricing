All prices in this app come from the National Average Drug Acquisition Cost (NADAC). CMS publishes this survey-based estimate of retail community pharmacies’ acquisition costs.

NADAC doesn’t show what you’ll pay at the pharmacy. It isn’t a patient’s price, a retail cash price or a reimbursement rate, so don’t use it to estimate your prescription bill.

Prices are **per unit**. EA means each, ML means milliliter, and GM means gram. For a price per tablet, multiply by the number of tablets to estimate the package’s acquisition cost. That’s still the pharmacy’s cost, not what a patient pays.

Read [NDCs, products and packages](/help/ndcs-products-and-packages) to understand the rows, or [Sources and disclaimer](/help/sources-and-disclaimer) for the data providers.
