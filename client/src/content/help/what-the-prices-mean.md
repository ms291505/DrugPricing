All prices in this app come from the National Average Drug Acquisition Cost (NADAC). CMS publishes this survey-based estimate of retail community pharmacies’ acquisition costs.

NADAC doesn’t show what patients pay at the pharmacy. A patient’s bill depends on the pharmacy’s retail price, insurance coverage and any discounts. Insurers may reimburse the pharmacy at a different rate.

Prices are **per unit**. EA means each, ML means milliliter, and GM means gram. For a bottle of 60 tablets, multiply the price per tablet by 60 to estimate what a pharmacy pays for the bottle.

When you compare NADAC with a figure of your own, such as a pharmacy invoice or an ingredient cost from a PBM report, convert it to the same unit and dates first. See [Comparing NADAC with your own figures](/help/comparing-with-your-own-figures).

Read [NDCs, products and packages](/help/ndcs-products-and-packages) to understand the rows, or [Sources and disclaimer](/help/sources-and-disclaimer) for the data providers.
