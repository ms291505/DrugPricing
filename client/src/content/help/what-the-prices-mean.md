All prices in this app come from the National Average Drug Acquisition Cost (NADAC). CMS publishes this survey-based estimate of retail community pharmacies’ acquisition costs.

Patient prices, retail prices and reimbursement rates are separate figures.

Prices are **per unit**. EA means each, ML means milliliter, and GM means gram. For a price per tablet, multiply by the number of tablets to estimate the package’s acquisition cost.

Read [NDCs, products and packages](/help/ndcs-products-and-packages) to understand the rows, or [Sources and disclaimer](/help/sources-and-disclaimer) for the data providers.
