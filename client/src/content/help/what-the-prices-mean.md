Every price in this app is a NADAC: the National Average Drug Acquisition Cost. CMS publishes this survey-based estimate of retail community pharmacies’ acquisition costs.

It is **not** a patient’s counter price, a retail cash price, or a reimbursement rate. Do not use it to estimate your prescription bill.

Prices are **per unit**, not per package or per dose: EA (each), ML (millilitre), or GM (gram). For a tablet priced per each, multiply by the number of tablets to estimate the package’s acquisition cost. That still does not tell you what a patient pays.

Read [NDCs, products and packages](/help/ndcs-products-and-packages) to understand the rows, or [Sources and disclaimer](/help/sources-and-disclaimer) for the data providers.
