Each example starts with a question someone brings to the app. DrugPricing supplies the published NADAC per unit. Figures from your plan’s reports, your pharmacy’s invoices or your own records are yours to bring. See [Comparing NADAC with your own figures](/help/comparing-with-your-own-figures).

The dates and prices below come from the weekly NADAC files. Your results can differ with the loaded data, dates and filters. If a previous search is on screen, choose **Clear search** before you start an example.

## Does NADAC show the gap in my PBM report?

Suppose your plan’s PBM report shows different ingredient costs for Mounjaro and Zepbound, two brands of tirzepatide.

1. Open **FDA search**, enter `tirzepatide` in **Generic name** and choose **Search**.
2. Under **NADAC price dates**, choose **All**.
3. Select the Mounjaro and Zepbound packages with a strength of 5 mg per 0.5 mL, then choose **Line**.

The legend names each line by package NDC: `0002-1495-80` is Mounjaro and `0002-2495-80` is Zepbound. Both pens hold 0.5 mL, and NADAC prices both per milliliter, so the two lines compare directly. Move along the chart to read the gap in any week. To compare a figure from your report, convert it to the same unit and the same weeks.

To check a year-over-year change, read both lines in the same week a year apart. On January 15, 2025, Mounjaro’s 5 mg pen was $2.64 per mL below Zepbound’s ($521.11 and $523.75). In the January 14, 2026 file, Mounjaro rose 3% and Zepbound didn’t, so Mounjaro was $12.67 per mL above it ($538.74 and $526.08).

If you have rebate information, you can also ask how close your ingredient cost net of rebates comes to NADAC. DrugPricing doesn’t import PBM reports, calculate rebates or show what a plan pays.

## Is the price per tablet the same in every package size or strength?

For package size, compare packages of one product. For strength, compare products of the same dosage form and pricing unit.

1. Open **FDA search**, enter `eliquis` in **Brand name** and choose **Search**.
2. Leave **Show** set to **Packages** so that each package has its own row and price.
3. Compare **Latest Price** for the packages of each **Strength**, then select one package of each strength and choose **Line**.

Eliquis 2.5 mg and 5 mg tablets have nearly the same NADAC per tablet: about $5.51 in late 2026. Package detail can show a difference that a product summary averages away. Try `zepbound` in **Brand name**: the single-dose pens have about the same price per mL at every strength, but the KwikPens, which hold four doses in 2.4 mL, cost about $201 per mL at the three lower strengths and about $282 per mL at the three higher ones.

Keep the unit with every number. Wegovy’s 0.5 mL pens are about $653 per mL and its 0.75 mL pens about $436 per mL, which comes to about $327 per pen for each.

## Did insulin prices move the way people expected?

Start with what you remember from the news. For example, several insulin makers cut list prices when the Medicaid rebate cap ended on January 1, 2024. Then check the published history around that date.

1. Open **NADAC search**, enter `lantus` in **Description** and choose **Search**.
2. Under **NADAC price dates**, choose **All**.
3. Select the Lantus vial rows and choose **Line**.

**Price Activity** shows that each vial dropped 78% on Jan 3, 2024, from $28.00 to $6.16 per mL. Search `humalog` or `novolog` to compare other insulins. The chart shows when NADAC moved and by how much. It can’t establish why.

## Where did the benchmark start for a new drug?

1. Open **FDA search**, enter `zepbound` in **Brand name** and choose **Search**.
2. Under **NADAC price dates**, choose **All**.
3. Select a package and choose **Line**. The line begins at the package’s first NADAC observation. **View data** under the chart lists it.

Zepbound’s 2.5 mg pen first appears in the January 17, 2024 file at $508.28 per mL. The first observation is the first week a NADAC file listed the package. It can come weeks after the drug went on sale, and it isn’t a launch price.

## How does what my pharmacy paid compare?

1. Open **NADAC search**, leave **Description** empty, enter the package NDC from your invoice in **NDC** and choose **Search**.
2. Under **NADAC price dates**, choose **Custom** and enter dates around the invoice date.
3. Divide the invoice cost by the units in the package, such as 60 tablets or 3 mL, to get a price in the same unit as NADAC.

Select the row, choose **Line** and move to the invoice week to read the NADAC for that week. NADAC averages invoices from many retail community pharmacies, so one pharmacy’s cost can be higher or lower.

## Keep a chart while you search again

Choose **Pin** on a chart to keep a copy in **Pinned**, then run another search to compare the new results with the pinned chart. To pin every chart you make, turn on **Pin charts when you make them** in Settings.
