Try these searches to explore the app. Results depend on the loaded data, dates and filters.

| Search | Where | Try this |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `eliquis` | either search | Compare the latest price with its history |
| `semaglutide` | FDA, Generic name | Inspect products and their packages |
| `nicotine` | NADAC, Description | Compare results with OTC drugs included |

Select priced rows and choose **Line** or **Bar** to chart them below the results. Choose **Pin**
on the chart to keep a copy in **Pinned**, then run another search to compare the new results
with the pinned chart. To pin every chart you make, turn on **Pin charts when you make them** in
Settings.
