To chart results, select their rows, then choose **Line** or **Bar** beside the results heading.
Each press adds a chart of the selected rows below the table, and a message under the heading
says what was added. **Show** beside it takes you to the chart. The selection stays, so you can
add a **Bar** of the same rows or change the selection and add another chart. A chart can show up
to 30 series, and more than eight can be slow and hard to read.

An added chart keeps its rows when you change the selection, and redraws when you change the dates
or filters. A row a filter hides drops out until the filter brings it back, and the subtitle counts
it as hidden. A new search, **Clear search**, or a change between **Packages** and **Products**
removes the added charts. Choose **Close** to remove one yourself (a chart with several pricing units has a card for each, and each closes on its own), or **Pin** to keep a copy that
stays as it is. To pin every chart you add, turn on **Pin charts when you make them** in Settings.

**Line charts** show price over time. The line moves in steps between weekly NADAC prices. A gap at the start or end means the app has no recorded price for that series in those weeks.

When a line stands for a product with several packages at different prices, it shows their
average, and a shaded band shows their lowest to highest price that week. The band is left out
when the spread is too thin to see on the chart; **View data** and the CSV still show the
range.

**Bar charts** compare drugs side by side, sorted by price. Switch between **Latest** price and
the **Average** over the chart's dates. Each row also shows the low-to-high range over those
dates.

**The readout** above the plot shows the date or bar under your pointer. Click or tap to lock
that point so you can scroll the readout. Click again, press Esc while the chart has focus, or choose **Unlock** to release it.

**The legend** hides and shows lines. Colors stay with their drug when you filter.

If selected drugs use different pricing units, such as per tablet and per milliliter, the app
creates a card for each unit, and each card's title ends with its unit, such as **USD per mL**.
Under the title, a line chart shows its dates, and a bar chart shows whether its bars are the
**Latest** or **Average** price and over which dates.
Older mixed-unit pins draw separate charts inside one card, each under its unit.

The line chart can fit its price axis to the data to show small changes. The app limits how
tightly the axis fits to keep changes in proportion.

**View data** under any chart shows its numbers as a table, and **Download CSV** saves them.

Line charts mark significant package price changes. A marker on a product chart means one or
more packages changed price. Find the package prices before and after in **Notable price changes**
or in the package chart’s **View data** table. The menu on a pinned line chart has a **Show price
changes** option. See [Price changes](/help/price-changes).
