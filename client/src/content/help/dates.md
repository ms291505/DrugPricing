**Dates** sets the period for results, live charts and price changes. Choose **Last year**,
**6 months**, **3 months**, **All**, or
**Custom** for your own range.

A new search starts with the last 12 months.

The app can adjust each boundary to a loaded weekly as-of date less than a week away. The
results show the dates used.

On FDA search, **Results without prices** includes entries with no price in the selected period.
Widen the dates to look for older prices.

Pinned charts keep their own dates. See [Pinning and comparing](/help/pinning-and-comparing).
