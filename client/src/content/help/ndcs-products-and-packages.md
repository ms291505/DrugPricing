An FDA package’s National Drug Code (NDC) has three parts.

| Part | What it identifies | Example (`0003-0894-21`) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Labeler | the company | `0003` |
| Product | the drug, strength and form | `0894` (Eliquis 5 mg tablet) |
| Package | the container type and size | `21` (bottle of 60) |

The first two parts together are the **product NDC** (`0003-0894`). One product can have several
packages. A brand can have several products, such as Eliquis 2.5 mg and 5 mg tablets.

A generic from another company has its own labeler and product codes. Read the full NDC to
identify the package.

FDA search shows **Packages** (one row per package) by default, or **Products** (one row per
product, prices averaged across its packages). NADAC search always lists
package NDCs. Both searches show prices per unit.

FDA search displays dashed codes such as `0003-0894-21`. CMS pads each segment to fit an
11-digit code, `00003089421` in this example. DrugPricing drops the leading zeros and shows
`3089421` on the NADAC page. You can enter any of these forms in the NADAC **NDC** field.
