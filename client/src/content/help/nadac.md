The National Average Drug Acquisition Cost (NADAC) is based on a survey of retail community pharmacies. CMS publishes updated data weekly.

It is a benchmark, not a quote from a particular pharmacy. Prices can change as survey responses and acquisition costs change. Not every drug package has a NADAC price.

The app shows weekly observations from the files it has loaded. A repeated price means the recorded price did not change between those observations.

See [CMS pharmacy pricing](https://www.medicaid.gov/medicaid/prescription-drugs/pharmacy-pricing) for the data and methodology resources.
