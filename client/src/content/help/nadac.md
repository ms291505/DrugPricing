The National Average Drug Acquisition Cost (NADAC) is based on a voluntary survey of retail community pharmacies’ purchase invoices. CMS publishes updated data weekly.

It is a benchmark, not a quote from a particular pharmacy. Prices can change as survey responses and acquisition costs change. Not every drug package has a NADAC price.

The app shows weekly observations from the files it has loaded. A repeated price means the recorded price did not change between those observations.

See [CMS pharmacy pricing](https://www.medicaid.gov/medicaid/prescription-drugs/pharmacy-pricing) for the data and methodology resources.

NADAC search lists package NDCs. The price is per unit (such as a tablet or millilitre), not
per bottle or box. FDA search can group those packages into products.

The survey does not cover every pharmacy or drug package.
See the [CMS methodology](https://www.medicaid.gov/sites/default/files/2024-03/nadacmethodology.pdf)
for the survey’s scope and calculation rules.
