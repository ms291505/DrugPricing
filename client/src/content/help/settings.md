Settings (the gear, or Settings in the Workspace menu) apply at once and are saved in this
browser.

- **Theme:** Dark or Light.
- **Pin charts when you make them:** choosing **Line** or **Bar** above the results also pins
  a copy that stays as it is. It is off by default.
- **Bar charts:** **Dot and range** marks each price with a dot on an axis fitted to the
  data, which shows small differences. **Bar and range** draws bars from $0, which shows true
  proportions.
